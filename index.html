01 / About

Engineering that connects analysis and hardware.

I am interested in the development and testing of propulsion systems for launch vehicles and spacecraft. At Oklahoma State, I work on an experimental liquid rocket engine, combining heat-transfer modeling, mechanical design, and hardware testing. My broader research experience includes ceramic thermal barrier materials at UT–ORII and cis-lunar orbital mechanics research at the University of Oklahoma.

I enjoy technical challenges where numerical models can be compared against physical experiments. I am exploring propulsion, thermal, mechanical design, and test engineering opportunities for Summer 2027.

Liquid Rocket Engine Cooling System

Leading cooling-system development for a small liquid rocket engine at Oklahoma State University, spanning water-cooled hardware concepts and regenerative cooling analysis.

SolidWorksPythonHeat TransferThermal-Fluid Analysis
Technical details
  • Analyze heat transfer and estimate combustion-chamber wall temperatures and cooling requirements.
  • Create SolidWorks models for chamber and cooling concepts, including a copper-tube and manifold configuration.
  • Use Python and engineering calculations to evaluate coolant flow, pressure drop, thermal performance, and manufacturability.
  • Preparing water-flow validation; Jet-A regenerative cooling remains a research and design objective.

Advanced Thermal Barrier Ceramics

Researched compositionally complex A₂B₂O₇ zirconate ceramics and thin films for thermal barrier coating applications at UT–Oak Ridge Innovation Institute.

RF SputteringCeramicsXRD / XRFSEM / EDS
Technical details
  • Used solid-state processing and 1600 °C sintering to synthesize ceramics and fabricate four sputtering targets.
  • Deposited thin films via RF magnetron reactive sputtering, varying power, Ar:O₂ ratio, and deposition time.
  • Characterized compositions and microstructures using XRD, XRF, SEM, EDS, and GSAS-II.
  • Presented work on bulk ceramics and thin films at a 2026 UT–ORII research symposium.
