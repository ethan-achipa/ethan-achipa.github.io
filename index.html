About Me

I am an Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering student at Oklahoma State University interested in liquid rocket propulsion, thermal-fluid systems, and spacecraft engineering. My experience includes rocket engine testing, thermal analysis, CAD design, orbital mechanics, and materials research. My long-term goal is to work as a propulsion engineer developing advanced spaceflight systems.

Engineering Projects

Liquid Rocket Engine Cooling System

I lead the development of the cooling system for a small liquid rocket engine at Oklahoma State University. My work includes heat-transfer and thermal-fluid analysis, evaluation of combustion chamber wall temperatures, coolant-flow analysis, and development of water-cooled and regenerative cooling concepts.

Tools: SolidWorks, Python, Heat Transfer Analysis, Thermal-Fluid Analysis

Propane-Oxygen Torch Igniter

Helped design, develop, and hot-fire test a reusable propane-oxygen torch igniter for a small liquid rocket engine. I analyzed oxidizer-to-fuel ratio sensitivity and ignition performance using experimental testing and mass-flow calculations.

This work contributed to the AIAA publication Development of a Propane-Oxygen Torch Igniter for a Small Liquid Rocket Engine Test Stand.

Skills: Rocket Propulsion, Hot-Fire Testing, Instrumentation, Data Acquisition, Mass-Flow Analysis

Cis-Lunar Satellite Constellation Modeling

Developed Python-based orbital mechanics simulations to model lunar satellite constellations and analyze coverage and communication availability for future lunar missions.

Tools: Python, STK, Orbital Mechanics

Materials Research

Advanced Thermal Barrier Coating Materials

Conducted research on A₂B₂O₇ zirconate ceramics for thermal-barrier coating applications at the UT-Oak Ridge Innovation Institute. Synthesized bulk ceramics, fabricated sputtering targets, deposited thin films using RF magnetron reactive sputtering, and characterized samples using XRD, XRF, SEM, and EDS.

Skills: RF Magnetron Sputtering, XRD, XRF, SEM, EDS
