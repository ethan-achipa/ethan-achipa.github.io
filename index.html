About Me

I am pursuing bachelor's degrees in Aerospace Engineering and Mechanical Engineering at Oklahoma State University. I am particularly interested in propulsion engineering and the development of thermal-fluid systems for launch vehicles and spacecraft.

My research has allowed me to work directly with liquid rocket propulsion hardware, perform engineering analysis, develop CAD models, conduct experimental testing, and communicate technical results through publications and presentations.

Featured Projects

Selected engineering and research projects focused on propulsion, thermal systems, orbital mechanics, and materials.

Liquid Rocket Engine Cooling System

Lead the development of the cooling system for a small liquid rocket engine. Perform heat-transfer and thermal-fluid analysis to evaluate combustion chamber wall temperatures and cooling requirements while developing water-cooled and regenerative cooling concepts.

I also develop SolidWorks CAD models and evaluate coolant-flow, pressure-drop, thermal-performance, and manufacturability tradeoffs.

Heat Transfer Thermal-Fluid Analysis SolidWorks Rocket Propulsion

Propane-Oxygen Torch Igniter

Helped design, develop, and hot-fire test a reusable propane-oxygen torch igniter for a small liquid rocket engine.

Evaluated ignition reliability and oxidizer-to-fuel ratio sensitivity using experimental testing and mass-flow calculations. The work contributed to an AIAA publication and conference presentation.

Hot-Fire Testing Mass Flow Instrumentation Data Acquisition

Cis-Lunar Satellite Constellation Modeling

Developed Python-based simulations to model lunar satellite constellations using orbital mechanics.

Analyzed constellation coverage and communication link availability for future lunar missions.

Python Orbital Mechanics Simulation

Advanced Thermal Barrier Coating Materials

Conducted research on A₂B₂O₇ zirconate ceramics and thin films for thermal-barrier coating applications at the UT–Oak Ridge Innovation Institute.

Fabricated ceramic sputtering targets, deposited thin films using RF magnetron reactive sputtering, and characterized materials using XRD, XRF, SEM, and EDS.

RF Sputtering XRD SEM / EDS Materials Research
